Appendix BSample Documents

The running example in full: one order, its acknowledgment, its shipment, its invoice, and the canonical form we map it to.

All five documents below belong to the same transaction — purchase order PO-88213, ACME Retail to Widget Supply, X12 005010, delimiters * element, : component, ^ repetition, ~ terminator.

B.1 — 850 purchase order, complete interchange

ISA*00*          *00*          *ZZ*ACMEBUYER      *ZZ*WIDGETSUPPLY   *260815*1430*^*00501*000000117*0*P*:~
GS*PO*ACMEBUYER*WIDGETSUPPLY*20260815*1430*38*X*005010~
ST*850*0001~
BEG*00*SA*PO-88213**20260815~
N1*ST*ACME RETAIL DC 7*92*DC07~
N3*1400 DOCK ROAD~
N4*COLUMBUS*OH*43230*US~
PO1*1*24*CA*18.50**BP*WID-1001*UP*000123456789~
PID*F****Widget, 10mm, boxed~
CTT*1~
SE*9*0001~
GE*1*38~
IEA*1*000000117~

Check the arithmetic, because it is exactly what a partner's validator checks: SE01 = 9 segments, counting ST, BEG, N1, N3, N4, PO1, PID, CTT and SE itself. CTT = 1 PO1 line. GE01 = 1 transaction set, IEA01 = 1 group.

B.2 — the matching 997

ST*997*0001~
AK1*PO*38~        acknowledging group PO / 38
AK2*850*0001~     …transaction set 850 / 0001
AK5*A~            accepted
AK9*A*1*1*1~       included / received / accepted
SE*6*0001~

And the rejecting variant, showing the addressing scheme chapter 8's findings imitate:

AK3*PO1*12**8~   segment PO1, position 12, segment-level error 8
AK4*3*234*1~     element 3, data element 234, error 1
AK5*R*5~         transaction set rejected
AK9*P*1*1*0~      group partially accepted

B.3 — 856 advance ship notice

ST*856*0001~
BSN*00*SHP-9021*20260817*0930~
HL*1**S~                      shipment
TD1*CTN25*2~
REF*BM*BOL-55120~
HL*2*1*O~                     order
PRF*PO-88213~                   the join back to the 850
HL*3*2*P~                     pack
MAN*GM*00000123456789012~     SSCC-18 on the carton label
HL*4*3*I~                     item
LIN**BP*WID-1001*UP*000123456789~
SN1**12*CA~
CTT*4~                        four HL loops
SE*13*0001~
The label must match the data

The SSCC-18 in MAN is the number printed in the GS1-128 barcode on the physical carton. If they disagree, the receiving dock scans a carton the ASN does not describe — which is a chargeback, and one of the more common ones in retail EDI.

B.4 — 810 invoice

ST*810*0001~
BIG*20260818*INV-40771*20260815*PO-88213~
N1*BT*ACME RETAIL*92*ACMEBUYER~
IT1*1*24*CA*18.50**BP*WID-1001~
TDS*44400~                    implied decimals: 444.00
CTT*1~
SE*7*0001~

BIG04 carries the purchase order number, which is what lets the buyer's accounts payable match the invoice to the order without a human. TDS is 24 × 18.50 = 444.00, written as 44400 — the implied-decimal trap from chapter 9.

Before you reuse these

Every sample here carries ISA15 = P. Copying a fixture into a test environment without flipping it to T is a genuinely common way to send a partner a test order they treat as real.
